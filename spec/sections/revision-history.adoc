| 2026-08-06 | 0.2.0 | *Where a curve stops, and what that means.*
                              `element { current_max }` becomes
                              `I_cutoff`, on an element and a stage; a
                              `view` keeps `current_min` /
                              `current_max`, which are axis bounds and a
                              different thing. One spelling had carried
                              two concepts. Breaking; the old spelling
                              is refused by name.

                              The cutoff stops being a drawing
                              instruction. It was read only where a
                              curve was rendered, so a high-set blocked
                              above the maximum through-fault was drawn
                              stopping at its ceiling and then evaluated
                              well past it: the composite kept returning
                              the blocked stage's 50 ms at twice the
                              current, and that figure was printed in
                              the grade report beside the sheet that
                              said otherwise.

                              A stage now does not operate past its
                              cutoff, so a composite steps back onto
                              whatever stage is still there and a
                              combined curve stops where its sources
                              stop. A grade *asked for* past a curve's
                              end is `GRADE_BEYOND_CUTOFF` -- refused,
                              not answered, because "grade at this
                              current" and "this curve ends before it"
                              cannot both hold. An upstream sweep is
                              clamped instead, being the tool's own
                              choice of currents rather than the
                              study's.

                              Error-severity grade diagnostics now join
                              the study's, so `check` exits non-zero on
                              a study it has refused to grade. They were
                              printed inside the text report and nowhere
                              else.

| 2026-08-06 | 0.2.0 | *Handles off the drawing.* A voltage level's
                              id is no longer printed: the axis, the
                              legend and every fault and condition
                              caption carry the figure (`33 kV`) rather
                              than the handle (`HV33 · 33 kV`). An id is
                              how a study *refers* to a level, and on a
                              study whose levels are called `L1` and
                              `L2` it said nothing at all.

                              A stage has no display name, so its id is
                              what appears; shown in upper case, it
                              reads as the designation it is --
                              `IDMT`, `HIGH` -- beside the `51` and `50`
                              it sits with. `data-ref` keeps the handle
                              exactly as written.

| 2026-07-28 | 0.1.0 | *Correction -- cross-voltage grading.*
                              The previous revision resolved the
                              `I_at_backup` contradiction the wrong
                              way, in favour of the worked examples
                              rather than the physics. Ampere-turns
                              balance across a transformer, so its
                              windings carry currents in inverse
                              proportion to their voltages; each relay
                              must be evaluated at the current *it*
                              measures, against its own pick-up.

                              * _Cross-voltage grading_ rewritten
                                around `I_at(L) = I_fault * V_fault /
                                V_L`, applied per relay. The claim
                                that the backup is evaluated at the
                                primary's current is withdrawn.
                              * _Worked grading case_ recomputed. At
                                `I_f = 6400 A` on LV the 33 kV incomer
                                measures 2133 A, giving `M = 2.963`
                                and `t = 1.912 s` (was `M = 8.889`,
                                `0.940 s`); margin `1.639 s` (was
                                `0.666 s`). The minimum-fault row is
                                recomputed likewise.
                              * _Worked solver case_ recomputed: the
                                backup measures 1616.7 A, giving
                                `bracket = 8.584` and
                                `TMS_b = 0.125` (was `0.290`).
                              * Margin reports now quote both
                                currents when a pair spans a
                                transformer, so the operate times can
                                be reconciled with the fault.

                              The old numbers understated the backup's
                              operate time and so reported grading
                              margins that do not physically exist --
                              a study would read as graded when it is
                              not. Implementations must adopt the
                              corrected values.

                              ambiguous when it did not, and invited a
                              phase current to be written where the
                              abscissa was a sequence component.

                                `current_pct` -> `share`,

                                `I_base`, `base_MVA` -> `base_S`,
                                `rating_A`/`rating_kV`/`rating_MVA` ->
                                `rating_I`/`rating_V`/`rating_S`, and
                                the `at_*` forms to match.
                              * Every old spelling is still recognised,
                                so it is refused with its replacement
                                (`RENAMED_KEY`) rather than looking
                                like a typo.
                              * A suffix is checked against the
                                quantity the field takes, not merely
                                against the union of all suffixes:
                                `I_pickup = 5 ms` is refused.
                              * `frequency_Hz` and `grounding` removed;
                                both were parsed, stored and read by
                                nothing, and `zero_sequence` declares
                                what `grounding` was reaching for.
                              * An unknown key warns rather than
                                erroring, so the sheet still draws; a
                                word the language knows nowhere, in a
                                block whose every field changes a
                                number, is a typo and is refused.
                              * *Exception, by decision:* the `page`
                                block's cosmetic keys keep their unit
                                in the name -- `margins_mm`, `size_px`,
                                `width_px`, `line_width_px`,
                                `label_size_px`, `tick_size_px`,
                                `label_offset_px`, `font_size_px` --

| 2026-07-31 | 0.1.0 | *Sheet controls.* Four additions, none of
                              which changes a computed number; all of
                              them change whether one can be read off
                              the drawing.

                              * `element { current_max }` stops a curve
                                at a stated current. A characteristic is
                                defined for any multiple, but the
                                network is not: past the largest fault
                                the bus can deliver, the curve describes
                                a current that cannot flow, and drawing
                                it invites a margin to be read at a
                                fault that does not exist. The legend
                                states the limit, so a curve stopping
                                short cannot be mistaken for one the
                                processor failed to finish.
                              * `times { at_I }` places a required
                                time's caption along its rule. The rule
                                spans the plot and so has no natural
                                anchor; the left-hand end is a default,
                                not an answer. A figure outside the
                                plotted currents falls back to it rather
                                than writing into the margin.
                              * Fault rules are drawn at full strength,
                                matching the legend swatch that names
                                them. At reduced opacity the rule read
                                as a different colour from its own
                                entry.
                              * Anything anchored outside the plotted
                                window -- a marked point, an annotation,
                                the leader running back to one -- is no
                                longer drawn. `toPx` extrapolates past

                                marks over its own legend. A point moves
                                to the legend list instead; annotations
                                are counted in the notes. Suppressed

                                the frame edge claims a current it does

| 2026-07-31 | 0.1.0 | *Correction -- cross-voltage curve
                              placement.* A curve belonging to another
                              level was positioned in two independent
                              steps: the turns ratio for the voltage,
                              then the depicted condition's ratios for
                              the quantity. The two disagree whenever a
                              scenario's per-level figures do not follow
                              the turns ratio -- and behind a delta they
                              must not, since phase current is
                              `I1 + I2 + I0` and the zero-sequence part
                              does not cross.

                              the LV breaker's curve a factor of
                              `sqrt(3)` to the right: its pickup fell
                              past the fault rule, so the sheet showed a
                              breaker that never operates while the
                              grading report had it clearing the same
                              fault in 1.631 s and passing.

                              * Where the named condition declares
                                figures at *both* levels, a curve is
                                placed from that condition alone -- one
                                factor for quantity and voltage
                                together -- and no referral is
                                performed. Plot and report agree by
                                construction.
                              * Where it declares only one (a `fault`
                                is one current at one level), the turns
                                ratio still applies, unchanged.
                              * `survivesVoltageReferral` answering
                                `true` for `phase` unconditionally is
                                retained for that fallback, but where
                                `zero_sequence` is declared `blocked`
                                between the levels and the condition is
                                unbalanced, the sheet states that the
                                placement rests on a ratio phase current
                                does not follow.
                              * A converted cross-level curve is stated
                                in the legend with both levels, the
                                factor and the condition, since a factor
                                carrying a change of level as well as of
                                quantity reads as neither alone.
                              * The same statement is made for
                                everything else the sheet refers --
                                fault rules, scenario rules, marked
                                points, annotations and condition
                                currents -- since all six sites gate on
                                the same referral rule and warning about
                                one while a silently misplaced marker
                                sits beside it would be a worse sheet
                                than either.
