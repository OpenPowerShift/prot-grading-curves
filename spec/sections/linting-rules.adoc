== Advisory findings

Findings the processor reports that are *not* refusals: the study
still grades and still renders, and the reader is told something worth
knowing about it.

Every code is listed with its meaning in
<<diagnostics,Diagnostic codes>>, which is generated from the
processor's own table -- so this section describes the *kinds* of
advice rather than repeating a list that would drift from it.

=== What is advisory and what is not

The distinction is what the finding does to the answer.

A *warning* leaves a computed answer standing and questions it: a
share declared twice where one wins, a dial the solver replaced, an
upstream sweep with nothing above the fault to sweep. An *info*
records a choice the study made implicitly, such as a fault stating
one current so its range endpoints default to it.

An *error* means there is no answer to give. A missing `tms` is not a
lint: nothing can be drawn without it, and defaulting it to 1.0 would
draw an IEC standard inverse about ten times slow and grade against
it. The same goes for a bare `I_pickup = 1.2` with no unit, and for a
curve id that is not in the constants table.

That line is drawn on whether the tool would otherwise have to invent
a number, and it is the reason this section is short.

=== The advisory findings the processor emits

*About the settings*

`SHARE_DECLARED_TWICE`, `PICKUP_TOO_LOW_FOR_VOLTAGE`,
`DEFINITE_NO_DELAY`, `ZERO_DELAY_NOT_PLOTTABLE`,
`FLEX_TIME_NOT_POSITIVE`, `RELAY_VOLTAGE_UNSPECIFIED`.

*About the grading*

`GRADE_MIXED_QUANTITY`, `MARGIN_FROM_DERIVED_COMPONENT`,
`MARGIN_NO_SOLVE`, `NO_OPERATION`, `FAULT_OPTIONAL_NO_GRADE_CHECK`,
`UPSTREAM_RANGE_EMPTY`, `UPSTREAM_WITHOUT_CTI`,
`UPSTREAM_DISABLED_WITH_CEILING`.

*About the solver*

`SOLVE_OVERRODE_SETTING`, `SOLVE_SUPERSEDED`, `SOLVER_NO_IDMT_STAGE`,
`SOLVER_NO_PRIMARY_OPERATION`, `SOLVER_NO_TARGET`,
`SOLVER_READONLY_DEVICE`, `SOLVER_UNSATISFIABLE`,
`MULTISTAGE_MULTIVAR_SOLVE`.

*About the drawing*

`CT_SPREAD_WIDE`, `SECOND_AXIS_SAME_LEVEL`, `ANNOTATE_LABEL_NOT_DRAWN`,
`SPAN_EMPTY`, `WATERMARK_ON_FINAL`, `UNKNOWN_KEY`.

[NOTE]
====
This section previously published fourteen rules as processor
behaviour, of which two behaved as written. Pickup discrimination
below 1.2, a backup with a steeper curve index than its primary,
monochrome print with thin lines, ABB RI/RD mixed with IDMT in one
pair, a fuse declaring one half of its band, and a vendor-specific
dial range all produced nothing at all. Two named codes
(`FUSE_CLEARS_BEFORE_RELAY_VIOLATED`,
`FUSE_AND_RELAY_BOTH_CURVE_ABOVE_CABLE`) were never emitted, and one
rule described `curve = si;` as "resolved via the disambiguation
table, but flagged" -- there is no such table and two hard errors.

They are not deleted as ideas: several are good ones, and they are
recorded as wanted work in `IMPLEMENTATION.adoc`. They are deleted as
*claims about what the processor does*, which is what this section
is for.
====
